# Privacy Policy for AI Buddy

**Last Updated: [DATE]**

## Overview

AI Buddy ("we", "our", or "the app") is a mental wellness companion application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data responsibly.

## Data Collection & Storage

### What We Collect
- **Chat Messages**: Your conversations with AI Buddy
- **Mood Entries**: Your mood check-ins and ratings
- **Journal Entries**: Your journal writings
- **App Usage**: Anonymous feature usage statistics (optional)

### How We Store It
- **All personal data is stored locally on your device only**
- **We use AES-256 encryption** to protect your data
- **No cloud storage** of personal conversations
- **No user accounts required** - your data stays on your device

## AI Processing

### How AI Works
- Your messages are sent to Google Gemini AI for response generation
- **Only individual messages are processed** - your full history is not shared
- Google processes data according to their [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- AI responses are generated in real-time and not stored by us

### What Google May See
- The text of individual messages you send
- Standard technical information (device type, app version)

## Data Retention

- **Chat history**: Automatically deleted after 90 days (configurable)
- **Journal entries**: Kept for 365 days by default
- **Mood data**: Kept for 365 days for trend analysis
- **You can delete all data at any time** from Settings

## What We DON'T Do

- ❌ We don't sell your data
- ❌ We don't share your data with third parties for marketing
- ❌ We don't use your data for advertising
- ❌ We don't track your location
- ❌ We don't access your contacts, photos, or other apps
- ❌ We don't require login or personal information

## Security Measures

- AES-256 encryption for stored data
- No server-side data storage
- No analytics identifiers linked to personal data
- Regular security audits
- PII (Personally Identifiable Information) is automatically redacted from any logs

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- **Access** your data (it's all on your device)
- **Delete** your data at any time through Settings
- **Export** your data (feature in development)
- **Opt out** of anonymous analytics

## Children's Privacy

AI Buddy is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used our app, please contact us.

## Crisis Resources

If you're experiencing a mental health crisis:
- **Emergency**: 911
- **Suicide & Crisis Lifeline**: 988
- **Crisis Text Line**: Text HOME to 741741

AI Buddy provides crisis resources but is NOT a substitute for professional help.
